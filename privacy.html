Privacy notice / 개인정보 처리 안내

Effective date: November 4, 2026. This notice describes the personal assistant connection named Hanbit Gmail Assistant.

Data accessed and purpose

Depending on the owner's requests and Google permissions, the connection accesses account identity, email content and metadata, labels, calendar events, Drive files, and document, spreadsheet, presentation and form content. This information is used to perform the owner's requested productivity tasks, such as search, summarization, organization and authorized updates.

Processing and service providers

The assistant runs in the owner's personal computing environment. Relevant task content may be sent to the AI model services configured by the owner to generate requested responses. Outputs or working records may be retained in the owner's workspace and connected storage. This does not mean all Google account data is uploaded to an AI service. This public information site does not receive or display the owner's Google account content.

Google data is not sold or used for advertising. Access and use are limited to the requested assistant functions and must comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google data must not be used to train generalized AI models.

Storage and controls

The shared OAuth connection uses the owner's protected macOS credential storage. Task records and generated outputs remain until the owner removes them or directs their removal; no fixed automatic deletion period is promised. The owner can revoke access in their Google Account third-party connections settings and remove locally retained records separately. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not itself delete prior outputs.

This website

This is a static GitHub Pages website with no sign-in form, analytics scripts or advertising added by the app operator. GitHub may process visitor information under its own privacy statement when hosting the site.
