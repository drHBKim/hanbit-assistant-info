Hanbit Gmail Assistant

A private, single-owner productivity assistant connecting the owner's Google account to their personal assistant environment. This is not a public sign-up service and is not affiliated with Google.

What it does

With the account owner's authorization, the assistant can find and summarize mail, organize messages, read and manage calendar events, and work with files and documents in Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Forms. Changes and sending messages are subject to the owner's task authorization.

Google sign-in and consent occur through Google. This informational website does not accept Google passwords, authorization codes, or account data.
